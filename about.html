About CDH Security

Providing excellence through experience

Dependable security services backed by experienced management, carefully selected personnel and a strong commitment to quality.

Who we are

CDH Security is an established security company delivering tailored security solutions across the United Kingdom. Our management team works closely with each client to understand operational requirements, assess potential risks and develop an effective security plan.

Our reputation is built on professionalism, integrity, reliability and excellent customer service. From a single short-term assignment to multi-site security coverage, we aim to deliver a consistent and effective service.

Our Mission

To protect people, property and businesses by delivering reliable, responsive and cost-effective security services, while building long-term client relationships through communication and tailored support.
